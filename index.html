The Game

One tab. Whole career.

Pick your sound. Pick your coast. The game remembers both, and so does everybody in it.

Songs that sound like records

You write in the room, hook first, off real life. The records you make carry the run, and the good ones follow you long after the session ends.

An economy that remembers

Every move lands somewhere. The money, the name, the catalog: they compound, they bite back, and no two runs balance the same way.

A city with a scene in it

A living map and a roster of named battlers with their own memories of you. Win the room and the city opens up, region by region.

The Musician

The game came from the records

DOUCET on every platform. Heavy on freestyle rap, cut live off the moment. Creole to the core, and the culture runs through every record.

The catalog: LOUISIANA CREOLE, the 2026 album, nine songs with Hay and LUKAS on it. Three EPs the same year: FLOW STATE, THE UNIVERSE SPEAKS JAZZ, EMOTIONAL CONTENT. The singles that started it: Hay and LUKAS, 2024. He makes his own beats about half the time, so the sound is his before the words ever land.
